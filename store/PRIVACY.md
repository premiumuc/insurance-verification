## Who we are
Healthy Companion ("we", "us") provides a free health and wellness companion app.

## The short version
- Your health data is **yours**. We store and transmit it in compliance with **HIPAA**.
- We **never** sell your data, and we **never** use your individual health data for
  advertising. Any advertising is contextual and non-personalized.
- You can export or delete your data at any time.

## What we collect
- **Account:** email, and (if you choose) name.
- **Health data you provide:** symptoms, mood, medications, sleep, nutrition, hydration,
  weight, menstrual/reproductive data, and related notes.
- **Connected device data:** with your permission, data from Apple Health, Android
  Health Connect, and wearables/CGMs you link (steps, heart rate, glucose, etc.).
- **Usage/diagnostics:** limited, PHI-redacted technical logs to keep the app reliable.

## How we use it
- To power your dashboard, reminders, non-clinical guidance, and contextualized
  (non-diagnostic) symptom information.
- To operate, secure, and improve the service.

## How we protect it
- Encryption in transit (TLS) and at rest; additional field-level encryption for the
  most sensitive data.
- Access controls, audit logging of data access, and Business Associate Agreements with
  our infrastructure and processing vendors.

## Sharing
- Only with vendors under a HIPAA BAA who help us run the service, or when you
  explicitly choose to share (e.g. exporting a visit summary).
- Never with advertisers at an individual level.

## Your choices
- Access, export, and delete your data in-app (Profile → account).
- Manage device connections and consents at any time.
- Contact: privacy@healthycompanion.app

## Not medical advice
Healthy Companion provides general wellness information and possibilities — **not a
diagnosis** and **not a substitute for professional care**. In an emergency, call your
local emergency number.
